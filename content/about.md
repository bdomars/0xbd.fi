Hi, I'm Björn, an infrastructure engineer based in Turku, Finland. Welcome to
my little corner of the internet.

I've spent the past decade building and running Linux production platforms,
mostly in small teams where you get to own the whole stack. I started out as
a software developer and still write Go, Python or Rust whenever there's a tool that
needs building. I simply enjoy programming, and Rust is my favourite language,
even if I haven't had much chance to use it professionally yet.

Outside of work I tinker. Usually that means small tools that scratch a
personal itch. I also run my own server, which hosts this site, my email and
whatever I'm experimenting with at the moment.

When I'm not at a keyboard, I'm probably at a different kind of key: I'm a
licensed radio amateur (**OH1DQQ**) and spend most of my on-air time on HF,
mostly CW (Morse code). I also play the fiddle, mostly traditional and folk music.

Some of the things I've built, or half-built, are on the
[projects page](/projects/).

If you want to get in touch, drop **bdomars** an email at this domain. ;)
